Color Block CO-OP

Slide every block into the door of its color, together, before time runs out.

or join a friend

How to play
  • Drag a block. It slides until it bumps into something.
  • A block leaves through a door of its color, but only if the door is at least as wide as the block.
  • Split colors mode: each player controls only their own colors, so talk it out. Shared hands: anyone moves anything.
  • Tap an empty tile to ping it for your team. Use the emote bar to call plays.
  • Watch for frozen blocks (thaw as others leave), arrow blocks (one axis only), layered blocks (two doors) and locks (open when the key block leaves).
